Sleep from sound

Suya lies next to you at night and listens: from breathing, movement and sounds it estimates your sleep stages, wakes you gently in light sleep and adds your night to Apple Health – no watch on your wrist needed.

Your nights stay on your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Only if you explicitly choose to, you donate a coarse sound picture of your nights – without audio – to make Suya more accurate for everyone.

Suya is not a medical device.
